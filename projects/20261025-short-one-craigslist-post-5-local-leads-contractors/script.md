## Description
A simple Craigslist playbook for contractors who do marketing themselves. Use one well-crafted post, one clear offer, and one follow-up step to get local calls.

This short shows the exact wording, an example for a plumber, and real steps you can copy. Costs are low and setup takes about 20 minutes.
