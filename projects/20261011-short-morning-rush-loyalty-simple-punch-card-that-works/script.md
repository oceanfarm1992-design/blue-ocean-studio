A tiny, low-cost loyalty trigger you can set up in one morning. Use a paper punch card that also gives a small, immediate upgrade halfway through the card to lock in repeat visits.

This short shows exact wording, staffing script, and a bakery example with rough cost so you can implement today. No fancy tech needed — just a stamp and a trained team member.
