# 1-Minute TikTok Script to Book Free PT Trials

Thumbnail text: **Fill Trial Slots Fast**

## Script

### 01 · HOOK
Fill every free PT slot in one week?
*Doodle:* trainer with empty calendar, worried · *On screen:* Fill Trial Slots Fast

### 02 · PROBLEM
You run a gym or train clients and free trial slots sit empty because people forget to book.
*Footage:* trainer at empty gym floor · *On screen:* Trials sit empty

### 03 · TIP
Send a one-line SMS that creates urgency and makes booking one tap. Keep it personal and time-limited.
*Doodle:* phone with a single message bubble and clock · *On screen:* One-line SMS

### 04 · EXAMPLE
Copy this: “Hey Sam — I’ve got a free 45-min PT trial Friday at 5PM. Reply YES to claim it.”
*Footage:* trainer texting client at desk · *On screen:* Reply YES to claim

### 05 · FOLLOW-UP
If they reply YES, send: “Great — what’s your best phone number? I’ll text a quick confirmation and the gym address.”
*Doodle:* trainer handing clipboard to a person, phone visible · *On screen:* Confirm details

### 06 · CLOSE
Film the message on-screen, show the empty slot filled, say: Follow Blue Ocean Marketing for one marketing fix every day.
*Footage:* trainer celebrating with new client · *On screen:* Slot Filled!

## Description
A super-short TikTok/Shorts script you can copy to book free personal training trials. Designed for trainers with little time and zero ad budget.

Includes a single, cheap tactic, exact words to send, and visuals you can film in one take. Use this to get more trial bookings without complicated funnels.
