Example: charge $1.50 for a cookie with coffee. Cookie cost is about $0.25, so profit roughly $1.25 each.

### 04 · SCRIPT
Train staff to ask: "Would you like a fresh cookie with that? It's $1.50 today." Say it cheerfully, not pushy.
